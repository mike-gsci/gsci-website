GSCI’s Strategy Advice

Who Can Make It?

Modern supply chains are increasingly complex, making it difficult to understand where manufacturing capability exists and where critical dependencies may arise.

Better supply chain decisions require more than supplier lists. They require an understanding of industrial capability, regional strengths and the wider manufacturing ecosystem.

GSCI combines manufacturing expertise, industrial intelligence and global trade analysis to help organisations better understand supply chains, identify strategic dependencies and support evidence-led decision making.

What Strategic Advice Delivers

  • Where manufacturing capability exists
  • Which industrial sectors can support your supply chain
  • Regional manufacturing strengths
  • Strategic supply chain dependencies
  • Opportunities to strengthen supply chain resilience
  • Potential areas for reshoring and localisation

Typical Applications

Supply chain mapping, regional capability assessments, supply chain resilience, reshoring & localisation, industrial strategy, infrastructure planning, procurement intelligence, economic development
