How Does the GSCI Model Make This Possible?

One Model, Four Data Layers. Unlimited Industrial Insight

01

The Challenge

Manufacturing businesses are classified using Standard Industrial Classification (SIC) codes, while international trade is measured using Harmonised System (HS) codes. These systems were developed for different purposes and do not naturally connect.

02

The GSCI Model

The GSCI Model bridges this gap by mapping manufacturing SIC codes to their equivalent HS product codes. This creates a direct link between manufacturing capability and international trade data.

03

The Result

By combining industrial classifications with global trade data, the GSCI Model delivers product-level manufacturing intelligence across more than 130 countries—revealing opportunities, dependencies and market trends that traditional industrial data cannot identify.
