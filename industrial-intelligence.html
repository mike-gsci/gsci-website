GSCI’s Industrial Intelligence

What Does Industry Actually Manufacture?

Most industrial statistics classify businesses by sector rather than by the products they manufacture.

This makes it difficult to identify industrial capability, investment opportunities and supply chain strengths.

GSCI combines manufacturing expertise, global trade intelligence and supply chain analysis to reveal the industrial capability hidden beneath traditional classifications.

What Industrial Intelligence Can Reveal

  • What products are actually manufactured
  • Where manufacturing capability exists
  • Which sectors are growing
  • Where investment opportunities exist
  • Which regions have specialist expertise
  • Where supply chain strengths and gaps occur

Typical Applications

Industrial strategy, investment promotion, manufacturing capability mapping, sector reviews, economic development, supply chain analysis, Government policy, infrastructure planning
